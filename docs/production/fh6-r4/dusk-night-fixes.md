# FH6 reference pass R4 round 3: dusk / blue hour, night marquees and start flares

Worker: agentL. Festival only (Solvane Coast); circuits, MotoGP and motocross keep
their day / golden hour / night unchanged (the shared `LightingMode` union, sky
shader and grade profiles are untouched; every dusk change is in festival
modules or behind a festival-only patch of the festival's own sky material).

Colours are sRGB hex of 9 x 9 patches of the 1920 x 1080 references (linear in
brackets), measured with `scratchpad/agentH/tools/stats.py`; "scene" values are
the radiance that gives the measured display colour through three's ACES at the
frame's exposure (`scratchpad/agentL/py/aces.py`). Game captures are 1280 x 720,
Medium, software GL.

Modules:

- `src/session/festival-lighting.ts`: `FestivalLighting` (day, sunset, dusk, night), `FESTIVAL_LIGHTINGS`, labels, `isFestivalLighting`,
  `festivalBaseLighting` (dusk runs on the shared night state),
  `festivalNightAmount` (lamps/windows/neon/headlights: 0 / 0.25 / 0.8 / 1).
- `src/rendering/open-world/festival-dusk.ts`: `FESTIVAL_DUSK` (measured dome,
  light, air), `DUSK_SUN` / `DUSK_KEY`, `installDuskSky` / `bindDuskSky` (the
  blue-hour dome patched into the festival's sky material: visible dome and
  every PMREM capture), `festivalDuskLight`, `duskLightColours`,
  `duskShadowAnchor`.
- Hooks: `FestivalSky.dusk` (festival-sky.ts: the dusk dome replaces the
  photograph; its key differs from the night photo's so the PMREM recaptures),
  `FESTIVAL_AIR.haze.dusk` / `FESTIVAL_GRADE.dusk` and the dusk branch of
  `FestivalAtmosphere.update` (festival-atmosphere.ts), the lighting wrapper in
  `FestivalRenderer.draw` (festival-renderer.ts), the pause-menu row
  (festival-hud.ts), `FestivalSave.lighting` (festival-save.ts, free-roam.ts),
  `?lighting=dusk` (e2e/fixtures/horizon-session.ts), the touge staged at dusk
  (region-data.ts).
- `src/rendering/open-world/event-dressing.ts`: marquee fabric shader, festoon
  bulbs (`TENT_BULB`), `tentPools`.
- `src/rendering/open-world/start-flares.ts`: rebuilt `FestivalStartFlares`.

The dusk dome costs nothing extra: under a full dusk neither the photographed
nor the procedural sky is evaluated (the dusk gradient and the 4 cloud fetches
replace them), and dusk adds no draw or render target. A switch to or from dusk
recaptures the sky PMREM once (as any lighting change).

Files: `scratchpad/agentL/before/` (hub-rows-night, hub-kestrel-night,
touge-start, spawn-sunset, pass-east-sunset, hub-kestrel-sunset, spawn-night,
