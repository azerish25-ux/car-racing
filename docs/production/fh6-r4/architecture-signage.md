# FH6 reference pass — architecture, landmarks, shop signage and night neon

Scope: festival region (Solvane Coast) only. Every structure keeps its collision
footprint, height and position (`regionStructures` is untouched); the Japanese
look is a presentation-only reading of those boxes (`japan-architecture.ts`).
Visual-only additions (castle keep, car-park markings, street furniture,
rear stairs) have no colliders and are kept off roads and other buildings by
construction and by tests (`tests/festival-japan-architecture.test.ts`).

Modules: `src/rendering/open-world/japan-architecture.ts` (style plan, store
lots, castle site, rear-stair clearance), `buildings.ts` (merged district meshes
and facade shader), `facade-layout.ts` (Japanese opening variants and wall
finishes), `facade-joinery.ts` (aluminium/concrete colours),
`festival-signage.ts` (canvas atlas + merged sign geometry), `horizon-dressing.ts`
(Japanese fascias, paper-lantern strings), `solvane-details.ts` /
`street-details.ts` (European kit parts withdrawn where they contradict the
Japanese reading).

## Measured dimensions used (real-world values where a photo is ambiguous)

- Kawara J-tile: 305 mm column pitch, 235 mm course; ibushi grey #2f3338-#4a4e53, roughness 0.58-0.72.
- Roofs: minka pitch 0.5 (26.6°), eaves 0.95 m, sori 0.1 m; machiya pitch 0.45, eaves 0.75 m, pent roof 0.85 m at the first-floor line; temple hall pitch 0.6, eaves 2.1 m, sori 0.55 m.
- Koshi lattice 30 mm slats at 60 mm; shoji kumiko grid 0.30 × 0.38 m.
- City storey 3.2 m (existing); window band sill 0.8 m, head 2.56 m, ≤2.3 m wide.
- Balcony 0.9 m deep, parapet 1.0 m + rail; AC condenser 0.80 × 0.55 × 0.29 m.
- Projecting signs 0.62-0.86 m wide, 0.26 m thick; vending machine 1.0 × 1.83 × 0.74 m; nobori 0.46 × 1.72 m on a 2.7 m pole.
- Store: wall 4.6 m, parapet 5.3 m, fascia 0.95 m, glazing modules 1.8 m, pole 9.7 m, sign box 2.2 m, bays 2.5 × 5.0 m, aisle 6 m.
