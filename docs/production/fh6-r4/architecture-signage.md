# FH6 reference pass — architecture, landmarks, shop signage and night neon

Scope: festival region (Solvane Coast) only. Every structure keeps its collision
footprint, height and position (`regionStructures` is untouched); the Japanese
look is a presentation-only reading of those boxes (`japan-architecture.ts`).
Visual-only additions (castle keep, car-park markings, street furniture) have
no colliders and are kept off roads by construction and by tests
(`tests/festival-japan-architecture.test.ts`).

Modules: `src/rendering/open-world/japan-architecture.ts` (style plan, store
lots, castle site), `buildings.ts` (merged district meshes and facade shader),
`facade-layout.ts` (Japanese opening variants and wall finishes),
`festival-signage.ts` (canvas atlas + merged sign geometry),
`horizon-dressing.ts` (Japanese fascias, paper lanterns over Harbour Front),
`solvane-details.ts` / `street-details.ts` (European kit parts withdrawn where
they contradict the Japanese reading).
