# FH6 reference pass r4 — festival road-car body form (agentG)

Scope: body shape fidelity of the festival's road-class cars (registry
`festivalVehicles()`, class `road`: eh3, lx12, rd2, rg63, rh3, rm8, rp6, rr4,
rs5, sc7), extended to the closed GT, GT-sport, club and touring cars the
festival admits (gt8f, gt10m, gt6r, gt6s, gs4, gs6m, cr1, hh2, tc1-3). All
shapes are original designs built from the construction principles visible in
the references; no real model's surfaces, badges or names are reproduced.

Gating: `FestivalCars` builds its library as
`new VehicleBodies({ festival: true })`; `buildVehicleBody(..., { festival:
true })` then uses `FestivalBodyModel`
(`src/rendering/vehicle-bodies/festival-form.ts`) for every car with a
`festivalFormFor` form. Circuit races, replays and ghosts build the plain
`ClosedBodyModel` and are byte-identical: `tests/festival-vehicle-form.test.ts`
locks the near-LOD geometry fingerprints (vertex/triangle counts and a hash of
every position at 0.1 mm) of all ten road cars and one race car of each closed
family (tc1, hy9, gt8f, gt10m, cr1) to their pre-change values. Collision and
physics data (wheelbase, track, wheel radius, half extents, wheel positions)
are untouched; wheels stay in their arches (tested: body side within 3 cm of
the tyre face, arch ceiling at least 2.5 cm over the tread at rest). Cockpit
eyes, onboard mounts and cabin enclosure are re-tested on every festival form
(`tests/vehicle-cabins.test.ts` runs its eye/mount checks on both builds).

Measurements are read on the 1920 x 1080 images (ruled 1280 px copies),
scaled by the Japanese plate (330 x 165 mm) where one is visible, else by the
car's width or wheel; they are approximate (perspective).

## Construction summary

- `src/rendering/vehicle-bodies/festival-form.ts` (new): `FestivalForm` (per
  style, `STYLE_FORMS`; per car, `CAR_FORMS`), `festivalFormFor` (classes
  road, street, gt, gt-sport, club, touring; rally, prototype and off-road
  keep the plain lofts), `FestivalBodyModel` (sections of 11 feature points
  with five duplicated crease points — floor edge, sill top / arch lip, door
  bottom, shoulder, hood/deck edge — so normals split along the character
  lines; LOD0 stations every 4.2 cm, 2 cm through the nose and tail wraps),
  `FestivalGreenhouse` (flush glass band with a crease at the glass top, a
  crowned roof tapering in plan, base following the cowl and deck), the end
  warp, mirrors, load bed, splitter, roof spoiler, corner vents, nose bar,
  chin.
- `closed-body.ts`: overridable hooks (`profile`, `sillX`, `mirrorAt`,
  `greenhouse`, `greenhouseMaterials`, `cabinStations`, `drawsWing`,
  `lampSetback`, `lampFace`, `endFloor`, `valanceHeight`, `warpGeometry`,
  `finishLod`, `addFormDetail`); the plain model returns its old values
  (fingerprint-tested).
- `festival-detail.ts`: `endBand` (bands proud of the surface along its plan
  normal), wrap tail lamps, proud rear bumper, refined fascia caps for the
  warp, flush/face-set lamp frames, sunk vents — all behind `model.festival`.
- `festival-car-finish.ts`: uses the body's own model (`body.model`), warps
  plates, running lights and halos with the body, plate on the bumper.
