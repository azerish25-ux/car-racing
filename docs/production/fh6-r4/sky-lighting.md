# FH6 reference pass r4: festival sky, clouds, sunlight, exposure and grade

Area owner: agentH (round 2). Festival only (Solvane Coast). Every shared-module
change is opt-in and defaults to the previous behaviour, so circuit, MotoGP and
motocross frames are unchanged:

- `photoClouds` (0), `photoTone` (gains 1) and `photoTint` (white) in
  `src/rendering/assets/photo-sky-shader.ts`; the sky fragment in
  `src/rendering/daylight.ts` composites the baked clouds over the photograph
  only when `photoClouds > 0` (test `keeps circuits on the photograph alone`).
- Everything else lives in festival modules: `open-world/festival-sky.ts`
  (`FestivalSky`, `FestivalSkyClouds`, `FESTIVAL_SKY`, `FESTIVAL_LIGHT`),
  `open-world/festival-cloud-shadow.ts`, two hook lines in
  `festival-atmosphere.ts` / `festival-renderer.ts`.

Cloud shares of the sky are estimated by eye (a saturation classifier
mistakes a pale clear gradient for cloud). Colours are sRGB hex of 9 x 9 patches of the 1920 x 1080 reference (linear in
brackets). Histogram figures are the Rec. 709 luma of the sRGB values
(percentiles) and the mean linear luminance (`Ylin`) and mean HSV saturation
of the whole frame, measured with `scratchpad/agentH/tools/stats.py`; game
captures are 1280 x 720, Medium, software GL.

## Lighting balance (measured, not guessed)

Fixed street view at the spawn (camera (552, 6.6, -2604) -> (760, 16, -2604),
so car settling does not change the framing):

|                                 | before        | clouds, diffuse 0.9 | final (diffuse 0.75) |
| ------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------- | -------------------- |
| sunlit asphalt (linear display) | 0.066 #474849 | 0.072 #4b4c4a       | 0.067 #484948        |
| shaded asphalt                  | 0.013 #1a1e26 | 0.017 #1f2228       | 0.014 #1c1f25        |
| sun : shade                     | 5.1 : 1       | 4.3 : 1             | 4.8 : 1              |
| frame Ylin / luma p50           | 0.189 / 0.37  | 0.172 / 0.30        | 0.159 / 0.28         |

- The clouds the festival shows over the simulated cover brighten the
  captured dome (open shade x2.4 in the first iteration, where the CPU dome
  model predicts x1.6). `festivalEnvironment` scales the diffuse IBL back to the
  dome of the simulated cover wherever the baked clouds are in the capture,
  times `FESTIVAL_LIGHT.diffuse` (day 0.75, set on this view; golden hour 0.9;
  night 1). The specular gain of `ibl-energy.ts` keeps reflections at the full
  dome, so paint mirrors the clouds.
- Day exposure is unchanged: lit asphalt 0.067 in game against 0.055 (012
  #44423d) and 0.048 (047). The frame stays darker than the midday refs (Ylin
  0.13-0.16 at the spawn against a median 0.29 over 001/002/012/018/020/021/
  036/039/047/049/082/094/095) because the spawn street is a canyon in shade;
  the open pass view reads 0.39 against 036's 0.36.
- Day grade is unchanged: frame saturation 0.25-0.26 against 0.19-0.32 in the
  midday refs; the pass view's 0.25 against 036's 0.32 is grass and forest
  albedo (terrain/vegetation), not the grade.
- Golden hour exposure x1.6 (1.62 -> 2.59): frame Ylin 0.15 -> 0.20-0.21,
  luma p50 0.26 -> 0.32-0.37 (refs 017/032/063/098: 0.27-0.34, 0.35-0.46).
- Night: unchanged exposure; the moonlit photo deepens to navy (frame Ylin
  0.12 -> 0.13; refs 072 0.084, 069 0.146).

## Cost

No draw calls or render targets are added per frame: the cloud panorama is the
dome's existing bake (one 3072 x 768 ray march when a cover bin or lighting
is first needed, bin 3 instead of bin 1 at the clear preset), the sky pixels
add the dome's 4 bilinear cloud fetches, lit festival fragments add the
2-fetch cloud-shadow term. Measured (same views, Medium, software GL):

| View              | before draws / triangles | after draws / triangles |
| ----------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| spawn-day (chase) | 425 / 2,456,512          | 426 / 2,462,196         |
| street (fixed)    | 425 / 2,662,750          | 423 / 2,662,062         |
| pass-east         | 590 / 2,723,510          | 591 / 2,706,102         |
| pass-east, Low    | 498 / 2,027,870          | 499 / 2,019,330         |

(+-2 draws and +-1 % triangles is traffic/streaming variance between runs.)

## Not done

- Blue hour / dusk (043, 061, 075, 081, 083): `LightingMode` is the shared
  day/sunset/night union (sky-light presets, PMREM keys, grade profiles, photo
  ramps, festival HUD and save). A festival-only dusk needs a sun below the
  horizon, a violet photographed or procedural dome in the captures as well as
  the visible sky, and its own lamp/ambient balance; documented, not faked
  with a visible-only sky tint.
- Season-driven skies (winter overcast 064): seasons do not change the sky.
- Cloud shapes and cirrus density are the shared bake's (studio/sky-clouds.ts),
  unchanged for the circuits.
