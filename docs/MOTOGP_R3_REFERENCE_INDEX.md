## What was and was not newly implemented

- `src/rendering/gp-cooling-louvers.ts` and `tools/motogp/author_gp_cooling_louvers.py` describe a new, original motorcycle cooling-outlet component; the production model installs it on the GP-R1 sprung fairing and retains built-in material/weather lifecycle. A still photo supplies form/material cues, not measurements.
- `src/simulation/bike-slipstream.ts` and the actual `BikeVehicle.step()` now consume the existing race-wide `slipstream` field; the 18% full-overlap drag-reduction ceiling is an authored gameplay parameter, **not** measured from these pictures.
- The R2 ledger remains the authoritative review for prior 74 partially applied, 10 preexisting and 16 context-only references. Neither R2 nor R3 establishes fully replicated scenes, licensed bikes, official MotoGP track accuracy, hardware frame rate or commercial PS5-quality fidelity.
