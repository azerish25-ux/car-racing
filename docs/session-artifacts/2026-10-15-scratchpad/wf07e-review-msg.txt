fix(offroad): review findings on tyres, wheelies, starts, jokers and the gate

Loose-ground tyres: the loose-surface recovery applies to grass and
gravel everywhere and to any loose material only on a terrain venue's
own ground (SurfaceSample.terrain, set by the course terrain). The
festival world's dirt, sand, snow and mud samples are back on the
original rule; test:open-world matches the base branch exactly.

Dirt bikes: the core's wheelie pitch is no longer added to the sprung
pitch at take-off. A held wheelie past its balance point over the
ground, with the drive still lifting the nose, loops the rider out on
the ground instead of launching the bike off flat ground. With the
wheelie aid (Sport, AI) the adapter reads the sprung chassis: a lifted
front closes the throttle, then covers the rear brake. The rider's body
fore/aft shifts the CG on the ground, and a slow climb up a steep face
stands the rider over the bars. A rider who comes off in the air
leaves a machine that keeps flying until it is down at its lying
height.

Jump rig: rolling a feature runs on distance (rollRun) past its end,
and the rig releases the rear brake on the ground as the AI does. Band
4 now rolls every gap jump down into the gap and out; the calibration
report is regenerated (the SKY TRIPLE hold run is clean without the
dragging brake).

Starts: one judge per start. The race director's lights check stands
down for gate and stage-control starts, and the format's penalty also
sets the lap's jump flag, so the penalty, the HUD and the results card
agree. The first stage starter waits at the control through the lights
and goes on the green; later starters are called 4 s before their
start. The snapshot carries a countdown and the HUD shows it.

Rallycross: a joker lap is three checkpoints on the joker road, in
order. Kestrel's joker road now has a turf island between it and the
lap. The joker requirement is applied as each car takes the flag, via a
race-director finish hook, so the last finisher pays too and the
classification includes it.

Menu and gate: a quick race at a terrain venue runs the venue's own
formatted ruleset (joker heat, special stage, moto). The rendered start
gate widens with the field, as the session's gate slots do.
