Circuit catalogue wave 2: 20 layouts, bike-safe circuits, long laps

Adds twenty layouts at seventeen venues to the circuit catalogue (40
layouts / 29 venues in total), appended after wave 1 so existing
geometry, sampling and seeded race results are unchanged:

- Motorcycle GP (bike-safe): Poggio Alto, Cape Wrenna, Haarveld,
  Montejaral.
- Endurance: Hochwald Forest Ring (20.8 km) and Loirette (13.6 km,
  plus Club Circuit), lit for day-night running; laps over 9 km get
  dense Track sampling (~4.5 m) and a matching contact ribbon.
- Real-world styles under neutral names: Serra Verde, Mount Ardlow,
  Arroyo Oro, Fiume Verde, Duinwijk, Ramla (night, two layouts),
  Mirvari, Lantern Bay (night), Lotus Hill.
- Unconventional: Gantry Point container-terminal figure-eight with a
  flyover and crane gantries (both directions), Bright Basin salt-flat
  airbase.

CircuitDefinition.bikeSafe marks motorcycle-assessed circuits; content
validation re-measures run-off, barrier distance, kerb height, banking
and frontage for every flagged circuit. The GP motorcycle and class
sprint rulesets and the road-bike career read the flag. Seven existing
layouts (Aurel, Vellamar, Parco Reale, Ardennes, Northfield, Kansai,
Texas Hill) are flagged without geometry changes.

Venue kit: twelve new themes, crane gantries, day-night floodlights,
theme style flags. Twenty wave-2 events; seeded AI races on all 40
layouts and two new seeded bike races pass. Docs: catalogue roster,
race table, provenance (unlicensed approximations), content pipeline
§2.3, status register.
