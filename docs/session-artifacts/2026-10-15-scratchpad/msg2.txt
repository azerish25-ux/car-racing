perf(open-world): cheaper surface queries on the road

The surface query scanned the road grid twice (nearest, then covering)
and evaluated every candidate. On a road the covering piece now answers
station and lateral itself (the nearest-centreline scan runs only off the
roads or for a query without a height), segments outside reach are
rejected on squared distance, and run-out candidates are evaluated only
when the point is not inside a carriageway. On 200,000 queries around the
roads (Node, best of three) this costs about 1.3x the nearest-centreline
query it replaced at d08eae5, down from 2.5x before this change.
