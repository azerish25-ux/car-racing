feat(vegetation): photographic trees, shrubs and meadow grass from CC BY 4.0 plant models

Replaces the procedural card crowns and cone conifers on HIGH and ULTRA
with plants baked from the pbrt-v4 Landscape scene (Laubwerk; Jan-Walter
Schliep, Burak Kahraman, Timm Dapper; CC BY 4.0, credited in-game with
the modification stated):

- Bake (scripts/assets/vegetation/, run by build-third-party.ts for the new
  "vegetation" recipe): pbrt object + PLY + PNG reader, leaf-area-density
  sky visibility through the crown, meshoptimizer trunks, a k-means
  billboard cloud of 80-480 leaf cards per plant (colour+coverage and
  tangent-normal+visibility atlases), eight impostor views, bark maps and
  meadow-grass clump slabs; KTX2 (bottom-first rows) + WebP fallbacks.
- Register: linden, horse chestnut, birch, rowan, two white firs, a
  flowering shrub and meadow grass, every source file pinned with its
  SHA-256; manifest and CREDITS regenerated.
- Runtime (src/rendering/vegetation/): per-venue species from the sky
  palette, PhotoForest instanced chunks with per-plant card/impostor
  crossfade, cards turned half toward the eye, wind sway and flutter,
  leaf transmission per shadowed light, baked crown occlusion, contact
  discs under crowns, alpha-to-coverage, light-facing impostors in the
  far shadow map; meadow grass along both barriers of every grass venue.
- Wired into circuits (Aurel layout, catalogue kits incl. off-road),
  forest belts, venue-kit shrubs and the festival tiles; LOW, MEDIUM and
  failed loads keep the procedural planting.
- Tests: tests/photo-vegetation.test.ts; docs: ASSET_PROVENANCE, D15 and
  risks, RENDERING_PIPELINE section 8b, STATUS_REGISTER.
