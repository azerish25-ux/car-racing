feat(offroad): terrain presentation, dirt-bike model, career, tests and races

Rendering (presentation only, materials library): the deformable course
terrain as a render grid posed from worker keyframes and kept for replays,
a soil PBR recipe with moisture darkening, roost particles, a mud coat on
bikes, riders and cars, the hinged start gate, the supercross stadium bowl
and an original dirt-bike model with long forks, high fenders, spoked
wheels and knobby tyres; the rider stands and dabs from the snapshot in an
off-road kit. Paved ribbons and kerbs are skipped on terrain venues.

The worker exports rut deltas with each surface keyframe; the HUD reads
gate, holeshot, joker and stage state. The career runs motocross,
rallycross and rally tiers at venues that admit their classes under the
venue's own formats. Dirt bikes join the machine menu and the vehicle
calibration. AI launches from the gate together and holds autopilot
players at the gate and start control. Unit tests cover jump ballistics,
landing windows, rut determinism with re-simulation, venues, gate
jump-starts, joker enforcement, stage intervals, moto points and the
terrain view; npm run test:offroad runs the calibration bands and seeded
races (docs/offroad-calibration.json, docs/offroad-races.json).
