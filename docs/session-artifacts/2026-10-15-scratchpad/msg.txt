fix(open-world): one continuous drivable surface at junctions, dead ends and verges

Junction mouths: sample() took the surface from the nearest centreline
only and tested the point against that one road's width, so the wide
road's asphalt next to a narrow road's centreline read as grass (451 m²
over 15 junctions, on the kestrel-circuit and runway-showcase lines) and
overlapping roads left steps of up to 1.04 m. Every road piece whose
footprint covers the point now counts, by true distance to each segment,
on the queried layer; inside a carriageway the highest road is the
surface (the ribbon the renderer shows on top), each road fades in across
its shoulder so overlaps meet without a step, and the surface class comes
from the carriageway the point is in (paved before loose).

Junction pads: roads meeting at a node are held level along each branch
until they are clear of the other roads' footprints (up to 40 m), side
roads following the through road's crown, with no superelevation across
the pad, so overlapping ribbons agree to within a crown instead of
diverging with their own grades (render and physics both read this).

Dead ends: the road ends at its end line; beyond it is the carved terrain,
no longer a flat phantom road plane reaching into the hillside.

Verges: the shoulder now falls to the verge edge the renderer draws
(no 6 cm kerb at the asphalt edge), and the ground meets the 4 m terrain
tile over a one-step run-out instead of dropping up to 43 cm where the
shoulder ends; the run-out stops at a neighbouring road's footprint.

Crossroads: a road end snapping to another road's end now also joins any
road whose carriageway it lies on, so valley, farm and orchard meet at the
four-way junction at (210, -430) instead of overlapping 35 cm apart.
validateRegion checks that every control point shared by several roads is
a junction joining all their branches.

Measured on Solvane: off-road area inside junction carriageways 451 m² to
0; adjacent 0.25 m height steps in junction areas over 15 cm 459 to 4,
worst 1.04 m to 0.17 m (a highway crown under a side road's shoulder);
shoulder-edge steps over 15 cm 2155 to 0, worst 43 cm to 4 mm (8 of
32,574 edge points, where another road's footprint touches, meet that
road: worst 10 cm); every event race line on road. Tests cover each.
