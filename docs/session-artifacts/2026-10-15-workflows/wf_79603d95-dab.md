**What exists now**
- **Disciplines:** Circuit cars is open. Road bikes, Off-road and Festival show as **COMING**, and each one lists what the registries are missing. Nothing is hard-coded: tests with a made-up registry show that adding a bike plus a race ruleset opens the bike career. A car class that no tier names goes into an automatic "open" tier.
- **Licences:** braking (stop on a marker), cornering (distance from the racing line) and wet control (largest slide angle on a wet lap) are playable and scored from session snapshots. The bike lean test and off-road jump test are written and tested on recorded samples, but stay coming until those machines, snapshot channels and venues exist.
- **Events:** each tier has invitationals (open once licences, reputation and an owned machine are in place) and a championship scored with the existing championship code.
- **Economy:** payments are itemised on the results screen: the existing `rewardIncome` lines plus improvement, low contact energy and objectives. Each session is paid once. Reputation is per discipline and cannot be bought. Prices follow class.
  - The **club meeting** is always open on a loaned car at quarter rates, so a player with no credits and no car can recover.
- **Garage:** owned cars, an active car per discipline, and setup presets that store only the parameters that car exposes, with plain-language effect hints.
- **Save:** one versioned career save, written with backup-before-overwrite. A corrupt or newer save falls back to the backup; with neither usable, a new career starts and the player is told. A first career is seeded once from the existing team, championship and setup saves, which are never rewritten.
- **Menu and session hook:** CAREER is now the first paddock button. Career sessions are built through `eventSession`, and a restart repeats the same career event.
